Practical Energy Saving Guidance

Actionable tips to reduce your bill and save electricity efficiently. Make smart choices for a greener future and a lighter wallet!

HVAC Optimization

Heating and cooling are the biggest consumers. Set your thermostat back 7–10°C (12–16°F) for 8 hours a day to save up to 10% on your HVAC energy bill.

Vampire Power

Devices like TVs, chargers, speakers still consume power when idle. Using smart power strips can cut 5–10% of passive electricity usage.

Appliance Scheduling

Run heavy appliances during off-peak hours (night/early morning). Helps reduce grid load and can lower your TOU electricity rates.

Water Heating

Set water heater temperature to 50°C (120°F) to reduce standby heat loss while maintaining comfort and safety.

Efficient Lighting

Switch to **LED bulbs** wherever possible. They use up to 80% less energy and last 25 times longer than traditional incandescent bulbs.
